Questions donors ask

Is my donation tax-deductible?

Yes. Wheels For Wishes is a tax-exempt charity under Section 501(c)(3) of the IRS Code, and you receive a tax-deductible receipt. Ask your tax advisor how the deduction applies to you.

Does my vehicle need to run?

Wheels For Wishes accepts most vehicles regardless of condition, and towing is free.

What can I donate?

Cars, trucks, motorcycles, boats, RVs and motorhomes.

We are a nonprofit

Proceeds from donated cars go to Wheels For Wishes, a tax-exempt charity under Section 501(c)(3) of the IRS Code, benefiting Make-A-Wish chapters in locations throughout the United States.

Car Donation Foundation also runs Vehicles For Veterans, benefiting disabled veterans; Wheels For Wishes & Wellness, benefiting local children's hospitals and charities; and Animal Car Donation, benefiting animal rescue organizations.
