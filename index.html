Check us out for yourself

Before you donate, you should know exactly who you're giving to and where the money goes.

  • A registered 501(c)(3) charity

    Wheels For Wishes, a program of Car Donation Foundation. EIN 26-3408048.

    Look us up on the IRS charity search

  • Your receipt matches the sale price

    Your tax-deductible receipt matches the full amount your vehicle sells for at auction. If it sells for under $500, you can claim fair market value up to $500. Ask your tax advisor how it applies to you.

  • Where the money goes

    On average, 39% of the auction value goes to Make-A-Wish. Towing and auction fees are fixed, so higher-value vehicles send a larger share.

Questions donors ask

Is my donation tax-deductible?

Yes. Wheels For Wishes is a tax-exempt charity under Section 501(c)(3) of the IRS Code, and you receive a tax-deductible receipt. Ask your tax advisor how the deduction applies to you.

Does my vehicle need to run?

Wheels For Wishes accepts most vehicles regardless of condition, and towing is free.

What can I donate?

Cars, trucks, motorcycles, boats, RVs and motorhomes.

How does pickup work?

Within 24 hours (by the next business day), you're contacted to schedule free towing. Your vehicle is picked up free of charge.

We are a nonprofit

Proceeds from donated cars go to Wheels For Wishes, a tax-exempt charity under Section 501(c)(3) of the IRS Code, benefiting Make-A-Wish chapters in locations throughout the United States.

Car Donation Foundation also runs Vehicles For Veterans, benefiting disabled veterans; Wheels For Wishes & Wellness, benefiting local children's hospitals and charities; and Animal Car Donation, benefiting animal rescue organizations.
